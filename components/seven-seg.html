Multiplexed 7-Segment Display

Three BCD registers share one decoder. A counter picks one register at a time and lights the matching digit, fast enough that all three look steady.

BCD to 7-segment truth table

Inputs 1010 to 1111 are not valid BCD. This decoder turns every segment off for them.

One decoder for three digits

The mux sends one BCD digit at a time, so a single decoder drives a segment bus shared by all three displays. That is 7 segment wires plus 3 enables, instead of 21 segment wires.

One count controls both ends

The same 2-bit count steers the mux and, through the 2-to-3 decoder, enables the matching display. Data and enable stay in step. Count 11 is never used: the counter returns to 00 after 10.

Refresh rate

Each digit is lit one third of the time, so with a clock of f each digit refreshes f/3 times a second. Drag the clock up and watch the three digits blend into one steady number.
