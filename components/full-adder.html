Half & Full Adder

A full adder adds three bits: A, B and a carry-in Cin from the column to its right. It gives a sum bit S and a carry-out Cout. Here it is built from two half adders. The first adds A and B. The second adds that partial sum P to Cin. An OR gate merges the two carries, because at most one of them can be 1.

The half adder

Adding two bits needs only two gates: S = A⊕B and C = A·B. The carry is 1 only for 1 + 1 = 102. It has no input for a carry coming in, which is why it is only "half".

The full adder adds a carry-in

Every column of a multi-bit sum except the rightmost one also receives a carry. A second half adder adds Cin to the partial sum P, and Cout = G1 + G2 = AB + Cin(A⊕B).

Parity and majority

S = A⊕B⊕Cin is 1 when an odd number of the three bits are 1. Cout is the majority function: it is 1 when at least two of them are 1. Check both against the table.
